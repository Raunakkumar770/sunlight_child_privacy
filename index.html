About Sunlight

Sunlight is a next-generation neighborhood delivery and alert ecosystem designed to streamline daily doorstep services without friction, noise, or constant phone calls.

The Problem We Are Solving

Every single day, vendors visit neighborhoods delivering essential services—from fresh water cans and morning milk to tiffins, laundry, and daily groceries. To alert customers, vendors traditionally resort to honking horns, blasting loudspeakers, or repeatedly making phone calls while navigating through streets. This causes unwanted noise pollution, missed deliveries, and unnecessary hassles for both customers and vendors.

Sunlight transforms this experience. With our smart real-time location triggers, digital subscriptions, and instant notifications, your local vendors can notify you seamlessly the exact moment they enter your street or arrive at your doorstep—quietly, accurately, and effortlessly.

Child Safety Standards & Protection Policy

Sunlight enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are dedicated to providing a safe, compliant, and trustworthy ecosystem for all users.

Prevention, Reporting & Compliance Standards

  • Zero Tolerance & Immediate Suspension: Any account attempting to share, transmit, or promote abusive, harmful, or inappropriate content involving minors will face immediate account termination and permanent blocking.
  • In-App Safety Mechanisms: Users can block or report inappropriate user-generated content, messages, or conduct directly within the application and via our support portal.
  • Reporting to Legal Authorities: We fully comply with applicable national and international child safety laws. Identified violations involving CSAM/CSAE will be promptly reported to relevant law enforcement agencies and safety authorities.
